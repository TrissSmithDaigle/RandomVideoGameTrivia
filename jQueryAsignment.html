What does HOTAS, HOTAM, and HOSAS mean, and what are they examples of?

HOTAS: Hands on Throttle and Stick
HOTAM: Hands on Throttle and Mouse
HOSAS: Hands on Stick and Stick

All three of these are examples of alternative Human Interface Device Control schemes meant to give greater control and/or imersion to gamers.
